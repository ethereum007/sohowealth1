# Role-language review

SoHo Wealth is described as an AMFI mutual fund and SIF distributor (ARN 306593) and APMI PMS distributor (APRN01233), not as a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser. Copy uses “review”, “compare”, “coordinate”, “support”, and “distribution” where accurate. Tax and legal work is assigned to appropriately qualified professionals. PMS rankings and samples are labelled educational, dated, sourced, and not recommendations. Unverified testimonials are not publicly rendered.
