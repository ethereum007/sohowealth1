# SEO and lead measurement plan

## Primary outcomes

- Qualified enquiry rate by intent and landing-page cluster.
- Scheduled-meeting rate, opportunity rate and won-client rate from `portfolio_leads` lifecycle stages.
- Organic entrances and non-brand search clicks to the canonical cluster owner pages.

## Events

`lead_offer_view`, `cta_click`, `lead_form_start`, `lead_form_step_1_complete`, `lead_form_step_2_view`, `lead_form_submit`, `lead_form_error`, `whatsapp_click`, `phone_click`, `email_click`, `booking_click`, `calculator_complete`, `pms_filter_use`, `pms_strategy_view`, and `sample_deliverable_view`.

Event parameters are non-PII: path, intent, keyword cluster, lead offer, CTA variant, component, service, result band, and error stage. Names, phone numbers, emails and free text must never be sent to analytics.

## Funnel reporting

Report weekly by intent: offer view → form start → step 1 complete → submit → qualified → scheduled → meeting completed → opportunity → won. Break down by first-touch source/medium/campaign and landing page. Investigate step conversion shifts larger than 20% week over week.

## Search reporting

Track the homepage plus owner pages for PMS, retirement, NRI, RSU, SIF and Hyderabad. Review impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, indexed pages and rich-result warnings monthly; annotate releases and data refreshes.
